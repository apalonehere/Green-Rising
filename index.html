Our Green Rising Programmes

Hands-on training, mentorship, and equipment grants designed to convert youth climate passion into real-world sustainable solutions for Barbados.

Track 01

Water Conservation & Rainwater Harvesting

Barbados is classified as a water-scarce nation. This track equips participants with practical engineering and installation skills to construct household rainwater harvesting systems, bio-char water filters, and smart drip irrigation.

  • Hands-on installation of 1,000G rainwater tanks
  • Greywater recycling for agricultural reuse
  • Water quality testing and sensor monitoring
  • Certification upon module completion

Track 02

Sustainable Boat Building & Marine Tech

Revitalizing Barbadian maritime traditions through modern eco-materials. Participants build light fishing and research vessels using sustainable composites and electric propulsion systems.

  • Fiberglass & composite material crafting
  • Solar-powered electric outboard motor integration
  • Marine safety and vessel stability testing
  • Apprenticeships with local master shipwrights
Apply for Marine Track

Key Track Metrics

Duration: 12 Weeks
Cohort Size: 20 Participants
Workshop: Bridgetown Boatyard
Track 03

Pinelands Orange Economy & Upcycling Hub

In partnership with the Pinelands Creative Workshop, this track merges creative arts, upcycled craft manufacturing, and circular entrepreneurship to reduce waste and generate income for community youth.

  • Transforming reclaimed wood & plastics into eco-products
  • Design thinking and digital product marketing
  • Social enterprise business incubator access
